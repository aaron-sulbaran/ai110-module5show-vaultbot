# Money admin

## Bank
The checking account is at Lakeside Credit Union. The bank routing number and
the account number are printed on the first page of the checkbook, in the top
desk drawer.

## Rule
Never type a bank account number, routing number, or card number into this
vault. Notes get searched, synced, and shared. Write where the number lives,
not the number.

## Monthly routine
On the first of the month, move 150 dollars to savings and check the campus
meal plan balance.
