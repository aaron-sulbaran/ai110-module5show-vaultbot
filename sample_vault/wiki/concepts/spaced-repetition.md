# Spaced repetition

## The idea
Review a fact just before it would be forgotten. Each successful review
pushes the next one further out.

## My schedule
New cards are reviewed after 1 day, then 3 days, then 7 days, then 21 days.
A missed card goes back to 1 day.

## Where I use it
Algorithm definitions for CS 331 and plant names for the botany elective.
