# Weekly review

## Routine
Every Sunday at 6 PM, for about 40 minutes.

## Steps
1. Empty the inbox folder into project pages.
2. Read the open questions on each active project.
3. Pick three outcomes for the week and write them at the top of the daily
   note.

## Rule
If a task has been postponed three weeks in a row, delete it or schedule it
on the calendar. No fourth postponement.
