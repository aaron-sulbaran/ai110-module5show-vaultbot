# CS 331 Algorithms

## Logistics
Lectures are Monday and Wednesday at 10 AM. The instructor is Professor Idris
Halloran.

## Grading
Homework is 30 percent, two midterms are 20 percent each, and the final exam
is 30 percent. The lowest homework score is dropped.

## Exams
- Midterm 1 is February 26 and covers divide and conquer and sorting.
- Midterm 2 is April 9 and covers dynamic programming and greedy algorithms.
- The final exam is May 12 and is cumulative.

## What clicked
Dynamic programming got easier after writing the recurrence in words before
writing any code.
